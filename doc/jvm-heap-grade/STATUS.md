  - **Pin-lifetime hazards:** (1) An open extent holds its epoch, so nothing retired after it can free. Measured: 100 swaps with an extent open left 99 blocks on the zero queue after a flush, and 0 after the extent closed. An extent must be scoped to a crossing, not a session. (2) Pins are keyed by thread id (pin-epoch!/unpin-epoch!). guard* takes and releases on one thread, but a receiving-domain lifecycle that closes on another thread would unpin the wrong entry and leak the epoch. (3) Raw mode has no check, so a raw value used after close is a silent use-after-free read.
